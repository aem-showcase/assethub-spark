❯ How to get DA token                                                           

⏺ DA (Document Authoring, admin.da.live) issues short-lived bearer tokens tied
  to your logged-in session there. To get a fresh one:

  1. Open https://da.live in your browser and make sure you're logged in (it
     uses your IMS/Adobe SSO).
  2. Open DevTools → Network tab, then trigger any action in DA (e.g. open a
     document, or just navigate a folder).
  3. Look at any request going to admin.da.live — check its request headers for
     Authorization: Bearer <token>.
  4. Copy just the token value (the part after Bearer ).
  5. Paste it into token.env in the worktree as DA_TOKEN=<value> (no Bearer 
     prefix, no quotes).

  Once that's saved, tell me and I'll retry the copy script.
